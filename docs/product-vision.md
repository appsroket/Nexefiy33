# Product Vision

## One-line

Nexefiy gives every business a supervised AI workforce: AI employees that know the business, use its tools, collaborate through workflows, and act only within the limits humans set.

## Problem

Small and mid-sized businesses want AI to do real operational work — answer customers, follow up leads, schedule meetings, publish content, reconcile records — but today they must stitch together chatbots, automation tools, and point AI products that:

- don't share business context with each other,
- can't safely take actions in business systems,
- give no clear supervision, approval, or audit trail,
- and can't show what was actually achieved.

## Vision

A single platform where an owner or operator can:

1. **Describe the business once** — products, policies, tone, FAQs, documents — and every AI employee uses that context.
2. **Hire AI employees** from templates (support, sales, marketing, operations, executive assistant, analytics, finance admin, HR admin, research, voice) or build custom ones.
3. **Connect business apps** (email, calendar, chat, CRM, helpdesk, accounting, telephony) through secure, auditable integrations.
4. **Assign work** directly ("follow up with every lead from this week") or through **workflows** created in natural language, edited visually, or configured manually.
5. **Supervise** — approve or reject restricted actions, see every step an employee took and why, pause or stop employees instantly.
6. **Measure outcomes** — tasks completed, response times, leads contacted, meetings booked, hours saved, cost per outcome.

## Core product principles

- **One runtime, many roles.** Every employee type is configuration over a shared runtime (instructions + skills + tools + policies + knowledge). New roles are templates, not new products.
- **Supervised by default.** AI proposes; policy and humans decide what executes. Autonomy is earned per tool and per employee, never assumed.
- **Real actions, honest status.** An integration either works and is tested, or it is clearly labelled as unavailable. No fake success.
- **Business context is a first-class asset** owned by the organization, portable across employees.
- **Outcomes over activity.** Analytics focus on business results, not token counts.
- **Tenant isolation is non-negotiable.** One organization's data, credentials, and runs are never visible to another.

## Target users

| Persona | Need |
|---|---|
| Owner / founder (SMB, 1–50 staff) | Get operational work done without hiring; stay in control. |
| Operations manager | Design repeatable processes, monitor execution, handle exceptions. |
| Team member | Collaborate with AI employees, approve actions in their domain. |
| Agency operator (later) | Manage AI workforces for multiple client organizations. |

## Long-term employee categories

AI voice employees, sales, marketing, customer support, analytics, operations, executive assistants, finance administration, HR administration, research, and custom employees — all sharing the employee runtime described in [ai-employee-architecture.md](ai-employee-architecture.md).

## What success looks like

- A new organization goes from sign-up to a working AI employee completing a real, approved task in under 30 minutes.
- Zero cross-tenant data incidents.
- Every external action traceable to an employee, a run, a policy decision, and (where required) a human approval.
- Customers can state, from Nexefiy's own analytics, what outcome they got for what cost.
